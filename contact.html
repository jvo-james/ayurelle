Common questions.

Still need help? Message us directly and we'll assist.

How do I choose the right product?

Start with what your hair needs most, such as cleansing, conditioning or a moisturising product. You can also contact us with your questions before ordering.

Do you deliver outside Accra?

Delivery options and fees depend on the destination. Contact Ayurélle to confirm availability, timing and cost before completing your order.

What if I have a question about my order?

Send us your order details by WhatsApp or email so we can review the request and explain the available next steps.

Can I order through WhatsApp?

Yes. You can use the WhatsApp links on the site to ask about products or review an order. Any prices, stock and delivery arrangements should be confirmed before payment.
